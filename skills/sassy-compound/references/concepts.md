# CONCEPTS.md rules

Read this before touching `CONCEPTS.md`.

- A term qualifies when a new engineer would need it defined to follow conversations, tickets, or code, and it is a concept in its own right (not a property of one already defined).
- One-sentence definition: what it means here, and what makes it distinct from its neighbors.
- **No** implementation specifics (paths, class names, signatures), no status/dates/owners, no versions, no thresholds or numbers, no external links.
- Cluster related terms. List dropped synonyms as `*Avoid: …*`.
- Be opinionated: pick the best word, drop the rest.
- Update, refine, or fold into a neighbor — do not duplicate an existing entry.
- The file stands on its own: each entry teaches its concept with no access to code, PRs, or meetings.
